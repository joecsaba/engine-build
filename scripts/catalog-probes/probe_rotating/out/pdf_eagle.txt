    WHAT’S NEW AT EAGLE
    4th GENERATION H-BEAM RODS
    The long-awaited next generation Eagle H-Beam rods is here! Our third design has
    been used for over 20 years with tremendous success. Our third design has been
    tweaked and improved slightly over the course of it’s life, but has never undergone
    a serious redesign until now. Our fourth generation (“4D” for 4th Design) features
    several design improvements around the big end bore to improve big end bore
    rigidity to help maintain a perfect shape in high rpm and high power applications.
    Also included is a thicker beam and webbing to handle even more power and load
    than before. The pin end is also strengthened. Eagle 4D rods are also 100% CNC
    machined eliminating any change for human error in manufacturing. Racers are
    making more power than ever with increased use of turbocharging and alcohol
    fuels. Eagle has risen to the challenge to meet those demands with our 4th
    Generation connecting rod design. Our new 4D rods will be sold along side our
    current production 3D rods in many applications.
